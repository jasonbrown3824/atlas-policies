Privacy Policy

Atlas is a personal planning and accountability application. This policy explains what information Atlas collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. It applies to the Atlas web application and to the SMS check-in messages Atlas sends to subscribers who ask for them.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the email address you use to sign in.
  • Mobile phone number — only if you choose to enable SMS check-ins.
  • Content you enter — goals, tasks, habits, schedules and notes you create.
  • Messages — the text of check-in messages sent to you and the replies you send back, so the service can maintain the conversation.
  • Calendar and task data — if you connect a Google account, Atlas reads your calendar events and task lists, and writes events or completes tasks when you ask it to. Atlas requests only the access needed for those functions.
  • Consent records — the date, time and wording of the consent you gave to receive messages.

How we use it

Your information is used solely to operate the service for you: to plan and display your schedule, to send the check-in messages you asked for, to respond to messages you send, and to make the changes to your calendar and tasks that you request. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Text messaging and your mobile information

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or your SMS opt-in data with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. Your consent to receive text messages is used only to deliver the messages you asked for, and is never shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

Message frequency varies according to the schedule you set. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, or by turning off SMS check-ins in the app. Reply HELP for assistance.

Service providers

Atlas uses a small number of providers to operate, and shares only what each one needs:

  • Twilio — delivers and receives text messages. Twilio receives your phone number and the content of those messages.
  • Supabase — hosts the database storing your account and content.
  • Anthropic — provides the language model that composes coaching replies. Message content and relevant goal context are sent to compose a response.
  • Google — only if you connect a Google account, and only for the calendar and task access you grant.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Retention and deletion

Your content and message history are kept while your account is active so the service can refer back to them. You may delete your content, disconnect Google access, or delete your account at any time. Deleting your account removes your content and message history.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls that restrict each account's data to that account. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Children

Atlas is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will change and, where the change affects messaging, subscribers will be notified.

The service

Atlas is a personal planning and accountability application. You set your own goals, habits and schedule. Atlas organises them, and — if you enable it — sends check-in messages about them and replies to messages you send back. With your permission, Atlas can also create or move events on your calendar and complete items on your task list.

SMS check-in messages

  • Opting in. SMS check-ins are off by default. You enable them by providing your own mobile number and agreeing to receive messages. Atlas then sends one confirmation message, and you must reply YES before any further messages are sent.
  • Message frequency varies according to the check-in schedule you set, and to how often you reply.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Charges from your mobile carrier are your responsibility.
  • Stopping messages. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe; you will receive one confirmation and nothing after that. You can also turn check-ins off in the app. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help. Reply HELP for assistance, or use the contact address below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
